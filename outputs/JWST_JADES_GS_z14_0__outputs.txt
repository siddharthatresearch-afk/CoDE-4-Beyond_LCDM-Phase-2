[INTERPRETATION]
The D_L^2 rescaling can lower an inferred stellar mass under the same SED assumptions. It does not, by itself, halve a cosmological formation-time tension.
In the legacy fixed-Omega_m comparison, the higher H0 makes the z~14 universe younger; the epsilon modification is small there.
A defensible abundance/tension claim needs halo abundance, survey volume/completeness, stellar-to-halo efficiency, and a self-consistent star-formation history.logy.
